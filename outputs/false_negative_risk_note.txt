AI-ASSISTED FORENSIC EVALUATION
FALSE-NEGATIVE RISK AND ANALYST ESCALATION
Case ID: ICDFA-DFIR-AI-026

1. OBSERVED TEST RESULT

The Logistic Regression model produced zero false negatives
in the 46-record test set. All 34 source-labelled positive
records were classified as positive.

This is an observed result on the supplied test data only.
It does not establish that the model will achieve zero
false negatives on unseen records.

2. POTENTIAL FALSE-NEGATIVE CONSEQUENCE

A false negative occurs when a source-labelled positive
record is incorrectly classified as negative.

In a forensic workflow, this could cause a potentially
relevant IP evidence candidate to be overlooked, excluded
from analyst review, or investigated later than necessary.

3. RECOMMENDED CONTROL

Automated classification must not be the sole basis for
excluding a candidate from forensic examination.

Preserve original source records and extraction context.
Apply independent IPv4 syntax validation.
Use analyst review for ambiguous or high-impact records.
Escalate uncertain cases for further contextual examination.
Record the reason for every analyst disposition.

4. LIMITATION

The supplied labels are teaching-dataset annotations.
They do not establish that a candidate is routable,
operational, malicious, or associated with an incident.

CONCLUSION

The model should be treated as an assistance mechanism,
not an autonomous forensic decision-maker. Human review,
evidence preservation, and transparent documentation
remain necessary safeguards.
